# HOA Management System

## Overview

The HOA Management System is a web-based application designed for managing the operations of **Iloilo River Plains Subdivision Village 3 Homeowners Association**.

The system provides a centralized platform for HOA officers to manage homeowners, monthly dues, payments, expenses, announcements, complaints, requests, projects, meetings, and community documents.

Homeowners can also access their own account information and relevant community information through the system.

## Features

### Homeowner Management

* Manage homeowner records
* Maintain homeowner account information
* Organize homeowners by block and lot
* Manage homeowner account status

### Dues and Assessments

* Manage monthly HOA dues
* Track homeowner assessments
* Support configurable assessment amounts
* Support future special assessments or community contributions
* Track outstanding balances

### Payment Management

* Record homeowner payments
* Track paid and unpaid dues
* Record payment dates and amounts
* Store receipt or reference numbers
* Maintain payment history

The system supports the existing cash payment workflow where homeowners pay the HOA secretary and receive a physical receipt.

### Financial Management

* View expected collections
* Track collected payments
* Monitor outstanding amounts
* Record HOA expenses
* View financial summaries
* Monitor project-related spending

Homeowners can view appropriate aggregate financial information while individual homeowner payment records remain private.

### Announcements

* Publish community announcements
* Inform homeowners about meetings, maintenance, water interruptions, security notices, garbage collection, dues reminders, and other community matters

### Complaints and Concerns

* Submit homeowner concerns
* Categorize complaints
* Track complaint status
* Monitor concerns from submission through resolution

### Projects and Maintenance

* Record community projects
* Track project budgets
* Monitor project expenses
* Track project status and dates

### Documents

* Manage community documents
* Store bylaws and rules
* Maintain meeting minutes
* Manage financial reports
* Store resolutions, notices, and project documents

### Meetings

* Manage HOA meetings
* Record meeting schedules
* Manage agendas
* Record meeting minutes
* Record attendees and decisions

### Requests

* Allow homeowners to submit HOA-related requests
* Manage requests from the HOA officer side
* Track request status

## User Roles

### Homeowner

Homeowners can:

* Access their account
* View their own dues
* View their own payment history
* Check paid and unpaid status
* View announcements
* Submit complaints and concerns
* Submit HOA requests
* View community documents
* View appropriate community financial summaries

### HOA Officer / Admin

HOA officers can:

* Manage homeowners
* Manage dues and assessments
* Record payments
* Monitor unpaid accounts
* Manage expenses
* Manage announcements
* Manage complaints and concerns
* Manage requests
* Manage projects
* Manage documents
* Manage meetings
* View financial summaries

## Purpose

The system is intended to provide the homeowners association with a centralized and organized way to manage community records, financial transactions, communications, concerns, requests, and other HOA operations.
